The complete core DL-native list is `revision_core_dl_papers.csv`, joined through
`corpus_id` to `analysis_papers.csv`. Each entry includes its available DOI, year,
venue, group and descriptive topic. The list identifies observed instrument
labels, not an independently human-adjudicated census.

`data/latex/numbers.json` records the display precision of the manuscript's
generated numeric macros. The raw CSV counts, not rounded typeset percentages,
are the basis of all recomputation. The filenames of the four main figures retain
their historical stems for source compatibility; current captions and this map
define the displays. The current manuscript assigns the human-calibration display
to Fig. 3 while retaining `fig4_penetrators` as its historical stem.
